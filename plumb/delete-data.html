PLUMB: Endless Tilt Maze · by rthDev · Android package com.rthdev.plumb

How to request deletion
  1. Send an email to rth.dev00@gmail.com with the subject PLUMB data deletion.
  2. Include your Analytics ID: in PLUMB, open Settings, tap Copy ID under Analytics ID, and paste it into the email. If you no longer have the game installed, tell us your phone model and roughly when you installed it instead.
  3. We confirm by email and forward your request to GameAnalytics within 30 days.

PLUMB has no accounts, so there is no account to delete. The data below is all the game produces.

What gets deleted

  • Gameplay analytics held by GameAnalytics for us (run events, session data, device details and the random installation identifier). Deleted or anonymised on request.
  • Your save file (progress, cosmetics, settings, purchase state). It exists only on your phone: uninstall PLUMB, or clear its storage in Android settings, and it is gone. We never have a copy.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Purchases are recorded by Google Play, not by us, and stay in your Google account so you can restore them. Manage them at Google Play order history.
  • Advertising data collected by Google AdMob is controlled by Google. Delete or reset your advertising ID in your phone's settings under Google → Ads, and manage the rest at myaccount.google.com.
  • Without a deletion request, GameAnalytics keeps gameplay analytics for as long as we use its service, then deletes or anonymises it. We keep nothing on servers of our own.
