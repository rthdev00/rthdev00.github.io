Privacy Policy

PLUMB: Endless Tilt Maze · Android package com.rthdev.plumb
Effective date: 28 September 2026

The short version
  • PLUMB has no accounts and no servers of its own. Your progress is saved only on your phone.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob. Google collects some device data to serve and measure those ads, as described below.
  • In-app purchases are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details.
  • We use GameAnalytics to see how the game is played (for example, how deep runs get), so we can balance it. It does not know your name or email.
  • We do not sell your data.

Who we are

PLUMB is developed and published by rthDev ("we", "us"). For any privacy question, contact us at rth.dev00@gmail.com.

Data stored on your device

PLUMB keeps one save file in the app's private storage on your phone. It contains:

  • game progress: best depth, run statistics, Cores balance, zones reached;
  • cosmetics you own and have equipped (skins and trails);
  • daily missions, streaks, the Daily Descent and weekly challenge results, and recorded "ghost" runs;
  • your settings (tilt sensitivity, volume, language, haptics and similar);
  • which in-app purchases you own, and how many optional rewarded ads you watched today (used to apply daily limits).

This file never leaves your device. It is not sent to us, and PLUMB opts out of Android cloud backup, so it is not copied to your Google account either. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

PLUMB is free and supported by ads served by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. It shows:

  • rewarded ads, which you choose to watch in exchange for an in-game reward (for example a revive or extra Cores);
  • interstitial ads, shown occasionally between runs.

To serve, limit and measure these ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects and processes data such as:

  • your device's advertising ID (and, on newer Android versions, the Privacy Sandbox advertising APIs);
  • the App Set ID, an identifier Android shares only between apps from the same developer on your device. It is used for analytics and fraud prevention, never for ads personalisation, and is not linked to your advertising ID for advertising purposes;
  • your IP address, which can indicate an approximate location;
  • device information such as model, operating system version, language and network type;
  • interactions with ads (views, taps) and diagnostic data about ad performance and crashes.

This data goes directly from your device to Google, which acts under its own privacy policy. We do not receive it in a form that identifies you. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your consent and ad choices

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: on first launch, PLUMB shows Google's consent form (the User Messaging Platform) before any personalised ads are requested. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy settings inside the game.
  • Everywhere: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's settings under Google → Ads (the exact path varies by device).
  • Remove Ads: buying the Remove Ads purchase turns off interstitial ads. Rewarded ads stay available because you only see them when you ask for one.

Gameplay analytics (GameAnalytics)

To understand how PLUMB is played and where it is too hard or too easy, the game sends gameplay events to GameAnalytics ApS, a game analytics service based in Denmark. Events include:

  • runs started and ended, with the depth reached and how the run ended;
  • floors cleared and how long they took, revives offered and accepted;
  • ads shown or failed to show, purchases made (product only, never payment details), and settings changed.

Along with these events, the GameAnalytics SDK sends a random identifier it generates for your installation, session start and length, and technical details such as device model, operating system version, app version, language and country. On Android it may also read the device's advertising ID. We use this data only to improve the game, never to show you ads.

No analytics data is sent until the consent step described below is complete, and in the EEA, UK and Switzerland only if consent was given. See the GameAnalytics Privacy Policy.

In-app purchases (Google Play Billing)

PLUMB offers one-time purchases ("Remove Ads" and the "Supporter Pack"). Payment is processed entirely by Google Play under the Google Payments Privacy Notice. We never receive your name, email address or card details. The game only receives confirmation from Google Play that a product was bought, and stores that on your device so the purchase keeps working.

Sharing a run

After a run you can tap Share to send a picture of your result. The image is created on your phone and passed to Android's share menu. It goes only to the app and person you pick; we do not receive a copy.

Children

PLUMB is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has used the game and you have a concern, contact us and we will help.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe or the CCPA in California), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data, or to object to its use. Your progress data in PLUMB stays under your control on your device. Gameplay analytics are linked only to a random installation identifier, not to your name or email; to have them deleted, contact us and we will ask GameAnalytics to remove them. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland you can stop further analytics at any time by withdrawing consent in Settings → Privacy settings; the game applies the new choice immediately. Elsewhere that option is not shown, so email us and we will have your analytics data deleted. For data collected by Google for advertising, use the controls above or Google's own tools at myaccount.google.com. You are always welcome to contact us with any request.

Data retention and security

Your save file stays on your device until you uninstall the game. Data sent to Google and GameAnalytics by their SDKs is transmitted over encrypted connections.

We keep no user data on servers of our own. GameAnalytics stores gameplay analytics for us for as long as we use its service to balance the game, then deletes or anonymises it, as described in its privacy policy. If you ask us to delete your analytics data, we forward the request to GameAnalytics within 30 days of receiving it. Advertising data is retained by Google under the Google Privacy Policy.

Changes to this policy

If we change how PLUMB handles data, for example by adding a new service, we will update this page and its effective date before the change ships in an update.
